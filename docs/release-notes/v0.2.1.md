The steering remote now saves its battery and tells you how full it is. **Download `V-Steer.exe` below** and replace your old one; your settings stay.

V-Steer 0.2.0 tells you about this release by itself: a notification and **Download** in the menu.

## The remote's battery

- **The remote can sleep again.** Before, V-Steer kept it awake for as long as it ran, even hours after the ride. Now, with no Zwift for 10 min, V-Steer lets it go, and the tooltip says *asleep until Zwift connects*. After pairing in Zwift, press any button on the remote once to wake it ([#2](https://github.com/valeraparafin/v-steer/issues/2)).
- **See the battery level.** Hover the tray icon: *Remote: connected 25%*. Zwift shows it too, as the battery on the **Controls** tile. At 15% or less, a notification tells you to replace the battery soon ([#36](https://github.com/valeraparafin/v-steer/issues/36)).

## Good to know

- **Pairing once per ride:** if V-Steer starts before Zwift (for example with Windows), pick **KICKR BIKE PRO 1337** under **Paired Devices → Controls** each ride. Zwift restores only Bluetooth devices by itself, not Wi-Fi ones like V-Steer, so this stays ([#10](https://github.com/valeraparafin/v-steer/issues/10)).
